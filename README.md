# 🎀 Partner's Little Corner 💗

A lively, hilarious, adorable, and deeply interactive surprise website for your partner! Designed with zero boring text reading, packed with games, teasing scanners, a Wheel of Shenanigans, Day/Night theme modes, action arcades, upbeat music, and heartfelt surprise letters.

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **☀️ / 🌙 Day & Night Mode (Theme Switcher)**:
   - **Day Mode**: Bright, cheerful pastel pink & rose blush aesthetic.
   - **Night Mode**: Dreamy starry midnight violet & neon pink glow with floating starry background particles.
   - Saves her choice automatically in browser storage with instant toggle buttons in the top navbar and mobile drawer.
2. **Welcome Screen 💗**: Glassmorphic opening card with mascot, warning message, particle sparkle burst, and unlock transition.
3. **Hero Section 🎀**: Mascot character, floating stickers (bows, hearts, stars, sparkles), and direct **"Explore The Fun 👀"** button.
4. **Photo Scrapbook 📸**: Clean Polaroid-style photo cards with washi tape, tilt angles, tags, dates, and a fullscreen **Lightbox modal** with keyboard/touch navigation.
5. **Funny Things You Do 😂**: 3D interactive flip cards revealing hilarious punchlines on tap.
6. **Forbidden Button & Special Partner Letter 🚨💌**:
   - When she presses the glowing **"DO NOT PRESS"** button, the site triggers a dramatic siren alert with screen shake and pops open a heartfelt, cute, and teasing **Partner Letter**:
     *"I SAID DO NOT PRESS IT... BUT OF COURSE YOU DID! 😂🎀"*
   - Full handwritten letter appreciating her chaos, humor, and irreplaceable presence.
   - Includes escalating teasing replies on repeated clicks and an instant re-read letter button!
7. **Boba & Treat Tap Arcade Game 🎮🧋**:
   - Fast-paced interactive arcade game!
   - Tap falling 🧋 Boba, 🎀 Bows, 💖 Partner Hearts, 🍟 Fries, and 👑 Crowns to build huge combo multipliers while dodging 🚨 Drama and ⏰ Alarms!
   - Calculates total score and awards certified ranks (SSS, S, A, B) with fanfare and confetti!
8. **The Partner Lie Detector & Tease-O-Meter 🔍🤖**:
   - **Biometric Thumb Scanner**: Hold to scan with live laser sweep animation and sound effects, revealing stats like *Stubbornness: 99.8%*, *Cuteness: 1000% (Illegal)*, *Drama Radar: 94.5%*, *Reply Speed: 12%*, and *Loyalty: 100/10*!
   - **Excuse Generator Soundboard 🔊**: Clickable sound buttons replaying her signature excuses.
   - **Interactive Scratch & Win Cards 🎁**: Real scratch-off silver/glitter cards to reveal hidden roasts and special perks!
9. **The Wheel of Shenanigans 🎡**:
   - **Interactive Spin Wheel**: Spin the wheel with realistic physics and ticking sound effects to land on hilarious partner dares, perks, and cosmic prophecies!
10. **Partner Awards 🏆**: Holographic trophy cards honoring lifetime friendship milestones with hover shine effects.
11. **Choose Your Fate 🔮**: 3 cosmic mystery boxes revealing funny randomized fortune prophecies.
12. **Upbeat Music Jukebox & Visualizer 🎵💿**:
    - Procedural upbeat multi-track synthesizer with 3 catchy songs (*"Pastel Pop Bop 🎀"*, *"Partner's Sunshine Groove 💖"*, *"Coffee & Chaos ☕"*) plus support for custom `music.mp3`!
    - Floating Jukebox controller with track name and dancing visualizer bars.
13. **Floating Interactive Mascot Pet 🐱🎀**:
    - Cute companion in the bottom corner that reacts with playful, teasing speech bubbles when poked or clicked!

## 🚀 How to Run the Website

### Option 1: Quick Local Launch (No installation needed)
Simply double-click `index.html` to open the website in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).

### Option 2: Host Online For Free
1. **GitHub Pages**: Push this folder to a GitHub repository and turn on GitHub Pages in the repository settings.
2. **Netlify or Vercel**: Drag and drop this folder directly into [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) or Vercel for an instant live URL!

## 🎨 How to Customize in `config.js`

All customizable text, photos, letters, jokes, and games are cleanly organized in **`config.js`**!

- `CONFIG.forbiddenSection.letter`: Edit the title, body, and signature of the forbidden partner letter.
- `CONFIG.arcadeGame`: Customize the items, durations, and rank titles.
- `CONFIG.teaseOMeter`: Customize the scanner stats, excuse soundboard, and scratch cards.
- `CONFIG.chaosStudio`: Customize the wheel items and dares.
- `CONFIG.scrapbook`: Add your real photos and custom captions.
- `CONFIG.funnyThings`: Edit the 3D flip card jokes.
- `CONFIG.awards`: Edit the trophies in the Hall of Fame.
